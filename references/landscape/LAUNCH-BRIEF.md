# Launch Brief — EKR Reference Landscape & Architectural Convergence

**Date:** 22 November 2026  
**Workstream:** RL / RA / AC / AI  
**Status:** Research coordination note — non-normative

## Mandate

Build and maintain a transparent, source-grounded reference landscape of standards, scientific work, architectures, specifications, platforms and methods relevant to Enterprise Knowledge Representation (EKR). Document possible convergence, divergence and complementarity without assuming EKR superiority or imposing EKR concerns on each reference.

The canonical conceptual reference remains *Enterprise Knowledge Representation — Working Definition V0.1* (DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21914714). Research outputs, including Article 2, do not automatically amend this anchor or become Guidance.

## Distinct outputs

1. **RL — Register:** permanent, broad, minimally described candidate inventory with immutable IDs. Indexing is independent of analysis.
2. **RA — Reference Analyses:** evidence-based profiles of individual references or families, distinguishing documented facts, claims, interpretations and unknowns.
3. **AC — Architectural Convergence:** thematic comparisons of concepts, boundaries, responsibilities, risks and demonstrable results.
4. **AI — Architectural Implications:** traceable candidate proposals, questions or counterexamples for EKR Guidance, experimental work and publications. Adoption is separately governed.

## Initial coverage

Ontological foundations; metadata registration and semantic alignment; industrial standards and integration; Data Mesh, contracts and Industrial DataOps; enterprise semantic/AI platforms; enterprise architecture, composability, agentic cooperation, action authority and evidence.

First cross-analysis: **ISO/IEC 11179 (notably 11179-3:2023/Amd 1:2026) and ANSI/ISA-95.00.07-2017**. Explore identification vs equivalence, mapping, governance and whether demonstrated cooperation requires further responsibilities. Do not presuppose the outcome.

## Working method

- Locate primary sources first; mark unverified source pointers explicitly.
- Describe each approach on its own terms before comparing it with EKR.
- Distinguish representation, mapping/mediation, technical interoperability, composition, execution, governance and evidence.
- Separate intended functionality, documented functionality, independent testing and operational evidence.
- Track both semantic commitments and their costs.
- Keep versions, scope exclusions and confidence explicit.
- Protect non-public IP, especially NRO/SPE and related multi-agent material, pending authorization.

## Coordination with EKR Publications & Trajectories

The landscape workstream owns research discovery and analysis. The publication/trajectory pilot owns editorial priorities, publication decisions and governed transfer to Architectural Guidance. Exchange **both directions**: research results and architectural implications to the pilot; targeted questions and priorities back to the landscape.

The launch itself changes no canonical EKR position, no Draft Guidance text and no FCP status.

## First review/acceptance conditions

- Existing references/evidence governance preserved.
- Public candidate register with stable IDs, coverage and uncertainty markings.
- Verified authoritative source pages for the two priority standards families where available.
- No confidential implementation disclosures.
- Draft PR reviewed before merge into the protected `main`.
- Next RA/AC work prioritized separately from accepting the register.
