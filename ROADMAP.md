# EKR Public Development Roadmap

This roadmap describes the **near-term public sequence**, not a fixed calendar or product roadmap. Timing is dependency-driven and may change as evidence and review require.

## 1. Canonical public anchor — complete

**Enterprise Knowledge Representation — Working Definition V0.1** was published on 14 August 2026.

Canonical version DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21914714

The archived Working Definition remains the canonical public definition. This repository links to it but does not replace it.

## 2. Repository and open-development mechanism — current

The public EKR workspace provides:

- governance and contribution rules;
- explicit artefact statuses;
- public canonicality policy and decision-record path;
- Discussions, structured Issues and pull requests;
- an EKR Architectural Guidance Working Draft V0.1;
- proposal, evidence, example and reader-aid routes for non-canonical development material.

The [EKR Architectural Guidance Working Draft V0.1](guidance/ARCHITECTURAL-GUIDANCE.md) is public with **Draft Guidance** status, not as Released Guidance or a reference architecture.

## 3. Article 1 — complete

**_Beyond Data: Why Knowledge Representation Is an Enterprise Architecture Concern_** was published in 2026.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22918368

Article 1 explains why access to operational data is insufficient for dependable enterprise interaction and positions knowledge representation as an enterprise-architecture concern. It provides context for later architectural investigation but does not replace the Working Definition.

## 4. Article 2 and FCP-PILOT-01 — published research inputs

Two research outputs from the investigation of distributed representations, shared participation commitments and semantic interfaces were published on 16 November 2026:

- **_Distributed Knowledge Representations, Shared Foundation and Semantic Interfaces_**, Version 1.0. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24284087
- **_FCP-PILOT-01: Candidate Foundation Conformance Profile — Investigation V0.4_**. Experimental, non-normative research note. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24284040

These publications introduce candidate material including F1–F4 commitment families, Foundation Conformance Profiles, Technology Binding Profiles, Technical Exposure Profiles, participation arrangements and an experimental evaluation programme.

Their publication does **not** by itself:

- revise the canonical Working Definition;
- establish F1–F4 as sufficient requirements;
- create a released FCP or certification scheme;
- confer Draft or Released Guidance status;
- designate any platform, ontology, API or technology as mandatory for EKR.

Candidate transfer into the public Guidance follows the repository Proposal → Issue → PR workflow.

## 5. EKR Architectural Guidance V0.1 — current development target

Continue developing the working draft through public questions, challenges, implementation experience, references, evidence and reviewed pull requests until it is ready for an explicit release review.

Its role is to turn stable EKR distinctions and reviewed research findings into architectural decision questions, minimum expectations, candidate patterns, evaluation criteria and explicit open questions.

It is not intended to prescribe one target architecture or technology stack.

Current research-transfer topics include:

- shared participation commitments and their minimum scope;
- independent and distributed conformance evidence;
- distinction among conformance, semantic fidelity, fitness and authorization;
- accountable cross-representation assertions and mapping-process representations;
- semantic-mediation boundaries;
- continuity of interpretation and accountability across platform replacement.

## 6. Evidence and independent testing — next

Develop non-normative cases, bindings and tests alongside the Guidance.

The immediate experimental priority is to test bounded FCP obligations through independent technology arrangements while keeping source facts and expected consumer observations controlled.

FCP-PILOT-01 proposes T01–T08 and X01–X04 but does not claim completed E2 implementation evidence. Independent assessment should therefore be treated as a next research step, not as a prerequisite that was already satisfied by publication.

Comparative work may include OPC UA and CESMII i3X bindings over the same expected observations, together with evaluation of third-party assertions, identity non-inference, temporal validity, restriction propagation and concordance of verdicts.

## 7. Comparative and convergence work — progressive

As evidence develops, continue comparing EKR responsibilities and candidate profiles with established standards, semantic-contract approaches, knowledge-graph architectures, data-product/data-mesh practices and governance platforms.

Such work should test whether an EKR-specific discipline adds measurable value beyond combinations of existing capabilities, and should identify overlap rather than assume a gap.

Potential convergence with EKG / EKG Foundation work, standards activities and implementation experiments may be pursued where useful evidence and public scope permit.

NRO remains a possible implementation or experimentation vehicle, not a mandatory EKR technology.

## Development principle

The public sequence is now:

**Working Definition → Article 1 → repository / Draft Guidance → Article 2 + FCP-PILOT research outputs → Proposal review → Guidance evolution → independent evidence and iteration**

The sequence remains evidence-driven. Publication makes research propositions citable and challengeable; it does not automatically promote them into Guidance or canonical EKR.
